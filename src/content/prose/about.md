## Where we are now

Human First AI is a group of volunteers, starting in Barcelona. We have just started. So far we have published [a few articles](/blog/), and we have panels, workshops, events and more in the works.

If you would like to help, [get in touch](/contact/?reason=help).

## What we believe

Nobody should be left behind by AI because of where they were born, how much schooling they had or how much money they have. People are less afraid of AI once they understand it. Everyone should have a say in how AI is used, including people who have never worked in tech.
