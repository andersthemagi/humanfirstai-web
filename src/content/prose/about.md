## Where we are

Human First AI is a group of volunteers, starting in Barcelona. We are at the very beginning. So far we have published [a few essays](/blog/), and we have panels, workshops, events and more in the works.

If you would like to help shape what comes next, [tell us](/contact/?reason=help).

## What we believe

Nobody should be left behind by AI because of where they were born, their schooling or how much money they have. Understanding it is the first step past being afraid of it. What happens next with AI should be shaped by everyone, including people who have never worked in tech.
