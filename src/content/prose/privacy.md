## What we collect

**If you ask for updates,** we keep your email address.

**If you send us a message,** we keep your name, your email address, what you chose under "What would you like to do?" and the message itself.

Both forms also note which language you were reading the site in, so we can write back in it. Netlify, the company that hosts this site and receives the forms, records the internet address (IP) and the browser each form was sent from. We do not use either.

Like most hosts, Netlify also keeps a short-lived log of visits to the site, which includes IP addresses. We do not look at it to identify anyone.

## What we do not do

- No cookies.
- No analytics and no tracking. We do not know who visits or what they read.
- No advertising.
- No fonts, scripts or images loaded from other companies. Everything on a page comes from this site.
- We do not sell your details or pass them to anyone else.
- We do not add you to a list you did not ask to join. Sending us a message does not sign you up for updates.

## Why we are allowed to keep it

For updates, because you asked for them (your consent). You can take that back whenever you like.

For messages, because you wrote to us and we need your details to answer.

## Where it is kept and who sees it

Addresses and messages are stored by Netlify. Netlify is based in the United States, so your details may be held there. [Netlify's privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) says how it looks after them.

The volunteers on our team read the messages. Nobody else does.

Our emails do not come from a newsletter service yet. If we start using one, we will name it here before any address is moved to it.

## How long we keep it

Your email address for updates: until you ask us to stop.

Messages: up to 12 months after our last reply, then we delete them.

## What you can ask for

You can ask to see what we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, or to stop receiving updates. [Write to us](/contact/?reason=other) and we will answer within a month.

If you think we have handled your details badly, you can complain to the Spanish data protection agency, the [AEPD](https://www.aepd.es/).

## Links to other sites

Some of our articles are published on GitHub Pages, and our team's profiles are on LinkedIn. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and what they collect is up to them.

## Who is responsible

Human First AI is a group of volunteers, starting in Barcelona. For anything about your details, [write to us](/contact/?reason=other).
