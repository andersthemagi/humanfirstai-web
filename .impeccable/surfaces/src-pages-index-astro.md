# Home (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: first-time learners first; volunteers and hosts second.
Action: newsletter subscribe ("Send me the letters"), Substack once its address is set, Netlify Forms until then, in the first viewport.
Proof available: none numeric. The movement is new; honesty about that is the proof.
Constraints: brand book palette/type/arch; no stock photos; no counts or impact claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a colonnade of the brand's arches. Each arch is a doorway you walk
through on scroll: Come to one, Help run one, Host one. Refuses the category default of a
centred headline over a row of three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm Cream ground, Deep Ink structure, Sunrise only as the light inside an opened
door and on the one primary button. Arches drawn at the mark's 30 : 21.5 : 15 geometry with
its 5-unit ink jamb and ground rule. Jakarta 800 headlines, Atkinson body. No cards, no
kickers, no icon tiles.

STORY: The visitor reads that AI is a tool, not a secret club, sees a door open, learns what
a session will be like, sees that helping or hosting needs no expertise, and leaves an email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An ink field, header included. "The door is open." in cream with "Come in." in
Sunrise beneath it, up to 96px, top-left. Standfirst and the newsletter form below at most 520px
wide. Bottom right, one large cream door on a cream floor that becomes the next section's ground;
it swings open on load, and two shut doors run off the right edge. On large screens scrolling
walks through the lit doorway until its light fills the screen.

FORM: Colonnade of doorways, position 5 of 7. seed:dbb24712c359395f. Raised by the paper
automata challenger (declined): one crank, one timeline. Every arch and door is driven by a
single scroll timeline, never independent reveals.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
