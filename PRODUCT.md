## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) + GSAP for motion, hosted on Netlify. Content lives as Markdown/JSON in
`src/content/`, edited through Sveltia CMS at `/admin` (GitHub backend, Netlify OAuth) so the
team can publish without going through a developer. Contact and "tell me when" sign-ups use
Netlify Forms. Chosen by Andrés: bootstrapped, $0/month is the target.

## Users

- **People who might come**: curious or anxious about AI, often unsure whether it is "for
  them". Job: find out, without being made to feel small, what this is and how to hear when
  something is on.
- **Volunteers**: people who want to give time. Job: see that there is room for them, and say so.
- **Hosts and partners**: libraries, community spaces, organisations in and around Barcelona.
  Job: judge whether this is a credible group worth opening a space to.
- **Press and policy**: need a clear position and honest limits.

## Product Purpose

Human First AI is a new volunteer group, starting in Barcelona, that wants AI to be for
everyone. The site's job is to say plainly what the group believes, show who is behind it, and
turn interest into a next step: people who want to hear when something is on, people who want
to help, and places that could host.

Vision: a world where AI creates opportunities for everyone, not just the privileged few.
Mission: make AI accessible, understandable and useful to everyone, especially those who need
it most. Promise: "AI for everyone. Opportunity for all." / "The future belongs to all of us."

## Positioning

Not a company, not a product, not an AI lab. Nothing has a price. The line that decides
everything: *Make AI feel like an open door, not a locked lab.* Success is measured in lives
improved, not in how many people use AI.

## Operating Context

The group is **just starting** (Nov 2026), in Barcelona for now. There is a core team of
five and three published essays. Panels, workshops, events and more are in the works, and
nothing has a date.

Decided by Andrés (13 Nov 2026), and binding on every page:

- **Say only what has happened.** Nothing on the site describes an event, a format or a habit
  that does not exist yet. What is coming is named once, in general: "panels, workshops, events
  and more are in the works".
- **No workshop specifics** (who brings what, how long, where, what it costs) until one is
  announced.
- **No mention of donations, funding or backing.**

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Home, About/why (with the team), Events, Writing, Contact.
- Every page must earn its place: would this make a visitor, volunteer or host want to
  take their next step? Every page ends on one.
- Events and team bios are edited by non-developers in the CMS.
- Must load well on slow mobile connections (the brand names rural 3G).
- **No claims of scale.** The "38 chapters" figure in the brand book is unsourced and must not
  appear. No workshop counts, attendee numbers or impact figures until real ones exist.

## Brand Commitments

Binding, from the brand repository (hkcarre/humanfirstai, `docs/`):

- Name is **Human First AI**, never hyphenated. "Human" leads, "AI" is smaller and quieter.
- Palette: Warm Cream `#FFF6EC`, Deep Ink `#1F2A44`, Sunrise `#FF8A3D` (fill only), Sunrise
  Deep `#C24D00` (accent text), Community Teal `#187F72`, Sky `#7CC4F2` (fill only), Sky Deep
  `#1277B7` (links), Ink Muted `#68728A`, Clay `#CE3E22` (the one alert colour). Never black
  glass, cyan or glow.
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans (brand, headings) and Atkinson Hyperlegible (anything read for more
  than a sentence). Nothing below 14px; body 17px+; never all-caps headings; never justify.
- One graphic device: the Open Threshold (the arch from the mark). No blobs, swooshes, mesh
  gradients, dot grids, robots, circuits or glowing brains.
- Voice: the neighbourhood teacher. Plain words, reading age twelve, no exclamation marks, no
  "unlock/revolutionise/seamless/AI-powered". Say what a tool cannot do in the same breath.

## Product Principles

1. Human leads: in the name, the layout and the sentence.
2. Honest about being early. An invitation, not a boast.
3. Nothing to sell. No upsell, no sign-up wall to read anything.
4. Readable by everyone before beautiful to anyone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA everywhere, AAA body contrast where achievable. 44px touch targets, visible focus,
colour never carries meaning alone, `prefers-reduced-motion` stops animation. Content renders
complete with JavaScript off; motion only enhances.
